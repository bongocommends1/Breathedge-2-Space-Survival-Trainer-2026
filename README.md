## 🎮 About the Game

Breathedge 2 is a humorous single-player survival adventure developed by RedRuins Softworks and published by HypeTrain Digital. It entered Steam Early Access on August 31, 2026, combining open-space exploration, resource gathering, crafting, repair systems, quests, and combat.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Breathedge 2**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for surviving and exploring Breathedge 2. It can stabilize oxygen and temperature, protect the player from common survival threats, simplify resource gathering and crafting, and improve combat encounters without altering the game's story or quest progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to focus on the story, exploration, crafting, repairs, and humorous space adventure while reducing survival pressure and combat difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the game's normal controls to navigate open space and reach abandoned stations, wreckage, settlements, and other optional locations. Unlimited oxygen and stable temperature are most useful during long excursions away from safe areas. Avoid activating survival options during scripted transitions or loading screens.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect wreckage, junk, and other resources normally, then use the inventory and crafting systems to produce tools and equipment. Unlimited resources and easy crafting are intended to reduce material management while preserving the game's blueprint and progression systems. Do not modify quest items unless the trainer specifically supports them.

### World & Environment

Explore gradually, inspect machinery, repair or dismantle objects, and search for routes into inaccessible areas. The Early Access version contains the first major story chapter, a large exploration area, quests, NPCs, optional locations, and core survival systems. Keep a recent save because future updates may change areas, items, and progression.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is only one part of the adventure, and some encounters can also be avoided by running away. Use unlimited health for safer exploration or one-hit defeat and unlimited ammunition when dealing with hostile enemies. Disable combat options before scripted scenes, boss encounters, or quest events if unexpected behavior occurs.
